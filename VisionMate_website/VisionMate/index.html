About VisionMate

Built to close the gap between sensing and safety

VisionMate is an IoT-based assistive navigation and safety solution designed to support visually impaired individuals in their daily journeys. By combining smart sensing technology with real-time monitoring, VisionMate helps users navigate their surroundings more confidently while enabling timely assistance when required.

  • Dual-channel output — voice and haptic feedback — built for deaf-blind users too
  • Live GPS and weather-risk awareness, refreshed continuously
  • One-tap emergency alert and call routed straight to the assigned caretaker
  • Zone-based caretaker dashboards for organized, scalable oversight
What's inside

Everything a caretaker needs, in one place

From live hardware telemetry to emergency response, VisionMate brings every signal into a single, calm dashboard.

Live location tracking

Real-time GPS on an interactive map, updated the instant the device reports a new position.

Instant emergency alerts

Button presses and hazard detection reach the assigned caretaker immediately — no delay, no manual checking.

Weather risk awareness

Forecasts and live conditions are checked against the user's location to flag risk before it becomes a problem.

Dual-channel accessibility

Voice and haptic output work together, so the system stays usable for deaf-blind users as well.

Zone-based caretaker view

Caretakers see only their assigned patients, organized by zone, with status and history at a glance.

Role-based access

Admins and caretakers each get a dedicated portal scoped to exactly what they're responsible for.
